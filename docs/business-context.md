# Business Context

This document describes the fictional organization used as the foundation for the Enterprise Infrastructure Lab.

## Company Overview

<strong>LexLearn</strong> is a software company focused on developing educational technology solutions.

The company has approximately <strong>80 employees</strong> distributed across multiple departments and requires a reliable and secure IT environment to support daily operations.

## Departments

- Executive
- Software Engineering
- Product
- Design
- Sales & Marketing
- Customer Success
- Finance & Administration
- Human Resources
- IT

## Business Requirements

As the company grows, LexLearn requires an IT environment capable of providing:

- Centralized identity management
- Secure access control
- File sharing and collaboration
- Device management
- Infrastructure monitoring
- Operational automation

## IT Environment Goals

The infrastructure should support:

- User lifecycle management
- Secure resource access
- Reliable internal services
- Scalability for future growth

## Future Growth

The environment should be designed to support future adoption of:

- Automation
- Monitoring solutions
- Security improvements
- Cloud services
